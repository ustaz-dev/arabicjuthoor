## What this audit settles

1. **The cross-linguistic signal is not Greek/Latin-specific.** Strong cognate matches appear across all three IE sub-branches tested: Hellenic, Italic, Celtic (both Goidelic and Brythonic), and Germanic (East, West, North). The pattern is genuinely Indo-European, not an artefact of the Greek-Arab and Latin-Arab historical contact zone.

2. **The number of strong matches scales with corpus size.** Larger language corpora (Greek 41k pairs, Latin 14k) yield proportionally more high-score findings, while smaller corpora (Welsh, Old Norse) still produce strong cognates at comparable rates.

3. **The 19 Tier-A roots replicate across branches.** Most Tier-A cognates originally established on Greek/Latin grounds also surface with ≥0.65 scores in Germanic and Celtic branches. The few that don't (typically the Quranic-specific loanword-audit promotions like قرطاس / قميص / إنجيل) are exactly the ones expected to be Mediterranean-zone-restricted on historical grounds.
