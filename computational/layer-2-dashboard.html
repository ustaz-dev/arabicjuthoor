Native composition
Methodology shift: v1 framed the third letter as a passive "modifier" of the binary, yielding ~95% with 10 doesn't-fit cases. v2 reframes the binary as the agent operating on L3-as-material — every native trilateral now has a relationship mode (CARRY, HOLD, RELEASE, etc.). The "doesn't fit" disappeared because the question changed: not "does L3 add up?" but "what does the binary do with L3's charge?"

Relationship-Mode Taxonomy

POSITIVE — binary co-acts with L3
NEGATIVE — binary opposes L3
TRANSFORM — binary reshapes L3

Interpretation

The v2 operative framing solved the v1 puzzle of "doesn't fit" cases. By reframing the binary as the agent operating on L3 rather than passively combining with it, every native trilateral becomes interpretable. The 12 relationship modes give a finite verb-grammar for how Arabic binaries interact with the third letter's material charge.

Key empirical findings:

  • Doubled-final roots (XYY) attract INTENSIFY heavily — the doubled letter is the binary amplified at its third-letter material.
  • Redoubled XY-XY roots show similar pattern — repetition signals intensification or repeated operation.
  • Letters with "mass/firm" charges (د, م, ق, ك, ص) attract OPERATE and HOLD — they're substances that get shaped or contained.
  • Letters with "flow/emit" charges (ر, ل, ن, ع) attract CHANNEL, CARRY, PROJECT, RELEASE — they're directional materials that get conducted or released.
  • Negative modes (BLOCK, DRAIN) remain minority across all letters — Arabic root composition is fundamentally constructive.

Method

For each trilateral, identify the binary nucleus (L1+L2), the L3 letter, and ask: what does the binary do with the L3-charge? Choose the dominant relationship mode from the 12-mode taxonomy. The reasoning chain captures the specific verb-relation: which binary axis interacts with which L3 face.
