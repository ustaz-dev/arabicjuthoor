### 1. ⲥⲱⲧⲙ (*sōtm*) / *sḏm* · "to hear, listen, obey"

*   **Coptic:** ⲥⲱⲧⲙ (*sōtm* - to hear).
*   **Egyptological Lemma:** *sḏm* (to hear, listen, obey).
*   **Phonosemantic Reading of the Lemma (*s-ḏ-m*):**
    *   **ⲥ / s (Streaming Flow):** The continuous streaming inflow of acoustic waves entering the ear.
    *   **ϫ / ḏ (Palatal/Emphatic grip):** Originally a palatalized voiced velar or emphatic sibilant. Represents catching, clamping, or stopping the stream to focus attention.
    *   **ⲙ / m (Gathered Mass):** Sealing and containing the captured sound within the mind/memory.
    *   **Synthesis:** *A streaming sound-inflow (s) caught and locked (ḏ) and gathered into the understanding (m)* $\rightarrow$ to hear, listen, or obey.
*   **Semitic/Arabic Alignment (سَمِعَ / s-m-ʿ):**
    *   **Arabic Root:** سَمِعَ / س-م-ع (to hear).
    *   **Phonosemantics of س-م-ع:** *Streaming inflow (س) gathered in (م) and perceived intensely at the deep throat/pharynx (ع)* $\rightarrow$ hearing.
    *   **Sound Shifts & Metathesis:**
        *   Sibilant `s` (س) $\leftrightarrow$ `s` (س) is a direct match.
        *   Nasal `m` (م) $\leftrightarrow$ `m` (م) is a direct match.
        *   The third radical: Egyptian `ḏ` (voiced palatal affricate/stop) corresponds to Arabic `ع` (voiced pharyngeal). Guttural/pharyngeal friction (`ʿ`/`ḥ`) regularly interchanges with palatal/emphatic friction (`ḏ`/`ṣ`) across the Afro-Asiatic family.
        *   **Metathesis:** The middle and final consonants swapped positions ($s-ḏ-m \leftrightarrow s-m-ʿ$), shifting the gathering gesture `m` to the center.
*   **Verdict:** **STRONG · METATHESIS & CLASS SHIFT**

### 2. ⲙⲉ (*me*) / *mꜣʿt* · "truth, justice, balance"

*   **Coptic:** ⲙⲉ (*me* - truth, justice).
*   **Egyptological Lemma:** *mꜣʿt* (Ma'at - truth, justice, cosmic balance).
*   **Phonosemantic Reading of the Lemma (*m-ꜣ-ʿ-t*):**
    *   **ⲙ / m (Gathered Mass):** The gathered, heavy, unified body or weight of truth.
    *   **ⲁ / ꜣ (Liquid/Glottal bridge):** Historically representing a liquid `r`/`l` in the earliest stages. Represents sliding alignment or balance.
    *   **ϩ / ʿ (Pharyngeal Grip):** Focused, absolute, unyielding depth or foundation.
    *   **ⲧ / t (Settled Finality):** Feminine ending or mark of completion.
    *   **Synthesis:** *A gathered weight (m) balanced and aligned (ꜣ) upon an unyielding, absolute foundation (ʿ) completed (t)* $\rightarrow$ cosmic balance, justice, truth (Ma'at).
*   **Semitic/Arabic Alignment (مَعَ / مَاعَ / م-ي-ع):**
    *   **Arabic Root:** مَعَ (*maʿa* - with, in alignment, alongside) and مَاعَ / م-ي-ع (to flow smoothly/freely, spread evenly).
    *   **Phonosemantics of م-ع:** *Gathered mass (م) in deep, focused union or alignment (ع)* $\rightarrow$ "together", "alongside", "in alignment".
    *   **Sound Shifts:**
        *   Coptic `ⲙⲉ` (*me* - truth) contracted the Egyptian root, dropping the glottal `ꜣ` and feminine `t` to preserve the core binary nucleus **ⲙ-ⲉ** (`m-ʿ`), matching Arabic **مَعَ** (*m-ʿ* - alignment/union) letter for letter.
*   **Verdict:** **PERFECT · CORE COGNATE** (Coptic preserves the bare binary `m-ʿ` nucleus representing alignment/truth).
