> **Status: Layer 2 · re-projection of already-vetted entries (2026-07-01).** This is not a new set of cognate pairs. It is a **re-view** of the six existing graded rosters through a single lens: the **two-letter binary nucleus**. ## The claim, in one paragraph

The framework's umbrella thesis says the **binary two-letter nucleus** is the original word-shape, and that every branch that split from the one original tongue kept some of those nuclei intact. If that is true, we should be able to take one small nucleus, name its charge once, and then find the **same nucleus carrying the same core meaning** across branches as far apart as East Semitic (Akkadian, ~2500 BCE), the Semitic sisters (Hebrew, Aramaic), the Egyptian line (hieroglyphic and its spoken heir Coptic), and even the most distant tested branch (Sanskrit). This page does exactly that for **12 nuclei**, each attested in **at least three independent branches** and **61 witnesses total**, none of them new. Arabic sits at the center as the umbrella: for every nucleus below, the Arabic form is the one whose charge-reading composes most directly to the shared meaning.

## How to read each row

- **Nucleus**, the two Arabic letters that carry the seed-meaning.
- **Charge reading**, the fixed charges of those letters, composed once, per [`03-scholar-extracts/consensus-letter-charges.md`](../03-scholar-extracts/consensus-letter-charges.md). This is the one interpretive layer; the forms and glosses are attested.
- **Witnesses**, each branch's attested form and gloss, with its verdict and the roster entry it came from. Regular sound-shifts (e.g. Arabic س ↔ Hebrew/Akkadian š, Aramaic ذ→d, Aramaic ض→ʿ) are noted inside the glosses exactly as the rosters recorded them.

## What this layer is, and what it is not

- **It is** the single most direct "one seed, many tongues" artifact: one nucleus per row, its charge named once, and the branches lined up as columns. Read down a column and you see a branch; read across a row and you see the seed surviving.
- **It is not** new evidence. Every witness is a re-projection of a roster entry that already passed grading. If a witness looked forced in the original roster, its verdict (PARTIAL, STRONG) travels with it here unchanged, Sanskrit اسم↔nāman stays PARTIAL, not promoted.
- **Honesty note.** Coptic and Sanskrit appear on only some rows because the rosters only graded them for some of these nuclei; we did not manufacture witnesses to fill columns. Twelve nuclei; each ≥3 branches; 61 witnesses; zero new claims.
