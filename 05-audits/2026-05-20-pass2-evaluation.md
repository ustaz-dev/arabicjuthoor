**Refinement discovered (not a failure):** Pass 2 surfaces three new augment categories that extend (rather than contradict) the pilot's table — the م-prefix (locative-instrument: مَنطِق, مِفتاح, مِنشار), the ت-prefix (action-derivational: تَنوِيم, تَكفير, تَوحيد, تَدبير), and the خ-final-pierce (in بَرزَخ). The augment-function table grows from 7 entries to 10 entries.

**Clarification on P3's "novel letter = FAIL" clause:** The pre-registration defined failure as "a novel **letter** outside the set {م, ن, ر, ل, و, ت, ـوت} appearing in the top-3 by frequency." This is distinct from "a novel **position** for an existing letter." The three additions above use م and ت — both members of the pre-registered set — in a NEW position (prefix rather than suffix). The خ-final discovery is in a single tail entry (بَرزَخ, position 135), nowhere near the top-3 frequency band. Therefore P3 passes on its strict letter-set test, while the position discoveries are recorded as a separate finding of the audit. No post-hoc relaxation of the criterion: the pre-reg condition is a letter-set test, and the letter set was respected.

## Updated augment-letter function table (after 50 T+A entries: 9 pilot + 41 Pass-2)

| Augment letter | Position | Function | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **م** (mīm) | final | gathers the trilateral into a *bounded mass / domain* | جَهَنَّم, إبراهيم |
| **م** (mīm) | **prefix** | derives the *locus / instrument* of the trilateral verb | مَنطِق, مِفتاح, مِنشار |
| **ف** (fāʾ) | final | parts the trilateral *onto a surface* (decoration, surface-display) | زَخْرَف |
| **ن** (nūn) | final | adds *resonance / emergence*, often personified | فَرْعَوْن |
| **ق** (qāf) | final | closes the trilateral into a *firm-bounded object* | نَمْرَق |
| **ت** (tāʾ) | **prefix** | derives the *action-state / causative-action* of the trilateral verb | تَنوِيم, تَكفير, تَوحيد, تَدبير |
| **خ** (khāʾ) | final | adds a *pierce / sharp-internal turn* to the trilateral state | بَرزَخ (the piercing-barrier) |
| **ـوت** | suffix (2 letters) | derives the *abstract state* (faʿalūt pattern) | جَبَرُوت, مَلَكُوت, طاغُوت, لاهُوت |
| **ـون** | suffix | derives the *permanent-state* | فَرْعَوْن, خَلَدُون |
| **ـان** | suffix | derives an *active state* (often as an established condition or recurring presence) | بُرهان, سُلطان, قُرآن, رَمَضان |

The table is now 10 entries (was 7). م and ت have a dual position (prefix vs final) with distinct functions in each — this is a structural finding of Pass 2.

## What Pass 2 settles

1. **Native-readability at the quadriliteral/quintiliteral layer is genuinely ~98%.** Two consecutive samples of 50 + 100 give 49/50 = 98% and 95/96 = 99%. The framework reads quadriliteral and quintiliteral roots at the same rate it reads trilaterals.
2. **The path-mix is structural.** Reduplication, B-on-B, T+A, and stacked all appear at stable proportions across both samples.
3. **The Quranic-native thesis extends to the quadriliteral/quintiliteral layer.** 36 Quranic-anchored entries across pilot + Pass 2 are all readable; the only UNRESOLVED case (zanjabīl) is path-competition not a framework failure.
4. **The augment-letter table generalises.** 21/21 = 100% consistency on overlap. Three new augment categories were discovered (m-prefix, t-prefix, خ-final) but they extend the table rather than break it.
5. **The mode-selection rule at the B-on-B level scales.** The interface-charge-selects-mode pattern holds across 50 new B-on-B entries (5 Pass 1 + 10 Pass 2 round 2 + 35 Pass 3) — interface-with-flow → CHANNEL/CARRY, interface-with-mass → HOLD, interface-with-sharp → OPERATE.
