> Method throughout: *find the Arabic word that describes the same thing the same way.* Don't accept "different roots." Try the rare word, the augment-stripped root, the metathesis, the articulator-class shift.

## Part B · Brand-new cognate pairs (entries 201–222)

These pairs are **not in the original 200-entry roster** — they were found by mining the Egyptian lexicon for words whose framework-reading and skeleton match an Arabic word. Several are exact three-consonant matches.

### Exact or near-exact (STRONG)

- **ḫsf** "to repel, punish, eclipse, cause to sink" ↔ **خَسَف** *ḫasafa* — **ḫ-s-f exact.** The Qur'anic خَسَفْنا بِهِ الأرض ("we made the earth swallow him") is the same punish-by-sinking image. ⭐
- **šnꜥ** "to repel, restrain, drive back" ↔ **شَنِئ** *shaniʾa* "to detest, repel" — **š-n-ʿ exact.** ⭐
- **ḥḏn** "to be angry" ↔ **حَزَن** *ḥazina* "to grieve" — ḥ-z/ḏ-n; the heavy negative emotion. ⭐
- **ḏrw** "limit, edge, summit" ↔ **ذِروة** *dhirwa* "peak, extremity" — ḏ-r-w. ⭐
- **ḥwt** "enclosure, house, temple" ↔ **حَوى** *ḥawā* "to enclose, contain" — ḥ-w; the bounded space. ⭐
- **ḫrw** "voice, sound, cry" ↔ **خُوار** *ḫuwār* "the lowing, the sound" — ḫ-r-w. ⭐
- **sḫm** "power, might (sekhem)" ↔ **ضَخم** *ḍaḫm* "huge, mighty" — ḍ-ḫ-m; magnitude. ⭐
- **wsr** "powerful, mighty" ↔ **أَزْر** *azr* "might, back-strength" (Qur'anic اشْدُدْ بِهِ أَزْري) — w/ʾ, s/z, r. ⭐
- **wꜣḥ** "to set down, lay, endure" ↔ **وَضَع** *waḍaʿa* "to put down, lay" — w + ḍ/ḥ + ʿ. ⭐
- **ḥꜣp** "to hide, cover, conceal" ↔ **خَفى/حَجَب** *ḫafā/ḥajaba* — ḥ/ḫ + f/p; concealment. ⭐
- **ḏfꜣ** "food, provisions" ↔ **ضِيافة** *ḍiyāfa* "hospitality-food" — ḏ/ḍ, f. ⭐
- **km** "black" (Kemet = "the black land") ↔ **كُمَيت** *kumayt* "dark-coloured" — k-m. ⭐
- **ḥfꜣw** "snake, serpent" ↔ **أَفعى** *afʿā* "viper" — f-ʿ; the shared Afroasiatic serpent-root. ⭐
- **ḫpr** "to become, come into being, transform" ↔ **عَبَر/غَبَر** *ʿabara/ɣabara* "to cross over / pass through" — transformation = transition. ⭐

### Plausible, wants your eye (PARTIAL)

- **pr** "to go out, go forth" ↔ **فَرّ** *farra* "to flee, go out" — p↔f, r.
- **snḏm** "to be pleasant, sweet, sit comfortably" ↔ **نَعِم** *naʿima* "to be soft, pleasant" — n-m.
- **ḳrs** "to bury, burial" ↔ **غَرَس** *gharasa* "to embed in the earth" — q/ɣ, r-s; burial = planting.
- **rn** "name" ↔ **رَنين** *ranīn* "resonance, the called" — r-n; the name as the called-out resonance.
- **ꜣbd** "month, moon" ↔ **أَبَد** *abad* "endless time" — ꜣ-b-d; the time-cycle.
- **ꜥꜣ** "donkey" ↔ **عَير** *ʿayr* (Heb. *ʿayir* too) — ʿ shared.
- **dgꜣ** "to hide" ↔ **دَجا** *dajā* "to become dark" — d, g/j; hiding = darkening.
- **ꜥnn** "to turn back, return" ↔ **عَنّ** *ʿanna* "to turn, appear" — ʿ-n.

## Part C · More PARTIAL cracks from the hard remainder

- **msḏr** "ear" ↔ **أُذُن** *ʾudhun* — the ḏ/ذ "inward-piercing channel" is the shared ear-nucleus (the sound entering inward).
- **m-ḫt** "after" ↔ **بَعد** *baʿd* via **خَتْم** *ḫatm* — "after = at the sealed end" (Egyptian builds *after* from *ḫt* "behind").
- **jtrw** "river" ↔ **نَهر/جَرى** — the **r** of running water; جَرى *jarā* "to flow" is closer than نهر.
